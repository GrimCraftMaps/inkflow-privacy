 # Inkflow Privacy Policy                                                      
                                                                                
  Last updated: November 8, 2026                                                
                                                                                
  Inkflow is a comic reader app for iPad and iPhone.                            
                                                                  
  ## Data Collection                                                            
                                                                  
  Inkflow does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All comic     
  files and reading preferences are stored locally on your device only.
                                                                                
  ## Data Sharing                                                               
   
  We do not share any data with third parties because no data is collected.     
                                                                  
  ## iCloud                                                                     
                                                                  
  Inkflow does not use iCloud or any cloud storage. Your files stay on your     
  device.

  ## Changes

  accept any changes.
